The user wants to build a web-based application for booking appointments for National ID registration services at the Uganda High Commission in London.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
- **User Flow:**
    - Users enter Surname, First Name, Email, and a UK or Uganda phone number.
    - Users select a service: Fresh Registration, Renewal of National ID, GetFirstID, Change of Particulars, or Card pick-up.
    - The system displays specific requirements based on the selected service.
    - Renewal and Card pick-up services require the user to enter their NIN or Application Number.
    - A calendar is displayed for selecting an appointment date and time.
- **Scheduling Rules:**
    - **Fresh Registration, GetFirstID, Change of Particulars, Renewal:**
        - Available Days: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.
        - Available Time: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM (UK Time).
    - **Card Pick-up:**
        - Available Days: Monday to Friday.
        - Available Time: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM (UK Time).
    - All weekends and UK/Uganda public holidays must be disabled.
- **Confirmation:**
    - After submission, the user receives an on-screen confirmation and a downloadable PDF letter.
    - An email confirmation is also sent.
    - Both communications must include: Applicant Name, Service Type, Appointment Date, Time Window (10:00 AM – 1:00 PM), a unique booking reference, and a notice about arriving within the scheduled timeframe.
    - A notice advising users to complete pre-registration on the NIRA website should also be included.
- **Manage Appointment:**
    - A Manage Appointment button on the home page.
    - Users can cancel or reschedule their appointment using their appointment ID.
- **Admin Panel:**
    - An admin login is required, with the button on the top right of the homepage.
    - Role-Based Access Control (Super Admin, Operations Admin, Front Desk).
    - **Appointment Management:** Search appointments, view details, reject with a reason (especially for 'Card Issuance'), and perform bulk rescheduling.
    - **Reporting:** Real-time dashboards (bookings, capacity), daily worklists, and downloadable reports (PDF/Excel/CSV).
    - **Audit Trail:** Immutable logs for all changes.
- **Technical/Design:**
    - Guest bookings only (no user registration).
    - The design must be mobile-first.
    - The cover page image should be of the Uganda House in Trafalgar Square.
    - Handle race conditions for simultaneous bookings.
    - Use SendGrid for emails and generate downloadable PDFs.
    - The final deployment URL should preferably contain niraservicesuk.
